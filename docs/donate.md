# Support BMuster

BMuster is free, open source and made by one person. If it saves you time, or your business relies on it, a small donation helps keep it maintained and improving. Thank you.

## Ways to give

- [**GitHub Sponsors**](https://github.com/sponsors/jackbrumley): monthly or one-off, through your GitHub account.
- [**PayPal**](https://www.paypal.com/donate/?business=NQLJU7FJPRFB4&no_recurring=0&item_name=Support+development+and+maintenance+of+Jack+Brumley%27s+free+and+open-source+apps.&currency_code=AUD): one-off or monthly, in Australian dollars.

Donations support all of Jack Brumley's free and open-source apps, BMuster included. They are gifts, not payment for support or features.

## About the reminder in BMuster

After a server has been in use for a couple of weeks, its admins see a short note at the bottom of the Machines page. It never appears for members, on the machines you support, or over your work.

- **BMuster sends nothing to anyone.** The reminder is built into the software and runs entirely on your own server.
- **It doesn't check whether you donated.** "I've donated, thank you" is on your honour: it hides the note for 90 days, and "Not now" hides it for 5.
- Only your own click on Donate opens this page.
